## What I collect

Only after you click **Accept** on the cookie banner:

- **Pages you view** on this site.
- **Clicks on email buttons**, recorded as which button was clicked (for example "hero" or "audit"). Your email address is never recorded.
- **Approximate location**, such as your country or city.
- **Device type**, such as phone or computer, and your browser.

I use this to see which pages people read and which ones lead to an email.

## How it works

This site uses Google Analytics. It sets cookies in your browser (named `_ga` and `_ga_` followed by an ID) only after you click Accept. Google keeps these cookies for up to two years unless you clear them. If you click Decline, or do not choose, Google Analytics does not load at all.

Your choice is saved in your own browser, not on any server.

## What I do not do

- I do not sell any data.
- I do not use it for ads. Google signals and ad personalization are turned off.
- The site has no forms and no accounts. The email buttons only open your own email app, and I see your email only if you send it.

## Changing your mind

Click **Cookie settings** at the bottom of any page and choose again. If you choose Decline after accepting, the Google Analytics cookies from this site are removed and nothing more is sent. You can also clear this site's cookies and storage in your browser settings.
