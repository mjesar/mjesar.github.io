Open-source contributions

While building product_geo_agent on the little_ghost Ruby agent gem with Gemini, I hit three bugs in the gem itself. I reproduced each one, reported it, and sent a fix. All three were merged upstream and released in little_ghost 0.11.0.

  • Replay Gemini thought signatures across multi-turn tool calls: Gemini expects its thought signatures back on later turns of a tool-calling conversation, and the gem did not send them.
  • Send the original function name in functionResponse.name: tool results went back to Gemini with the call id instead of the function name.
  • Preserve Agent lifecycle hooks when building execution snapshots: hooks on an Agent subclass were silently skipped, with no error and a normal-looking response.

I also filed ruby_llm-mcp#155 after the gem crashed on Shopify's MCP handshake response.

Past work: Shopify and BigCommerce apps

In my previous role I was part of the team that built and shipped five public Shopify apps (three also on BigCommerce), from architecture through App Store publishing and merchant support. They covered:

I also built an Amazon Import & Sync System on Amazon SP-API that keeps listings, variants and images in step between Amazon and e-commerce stores (client work, not public).
