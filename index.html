About

I am a software engineer with 7+ years of experience building and maintaining production applications for international clients. My core expertise is Ruby on Rails, full-stack development, and integrating AI into practical software.

I help businesses turn ideas into working products and SaaS applications, improve existing software, and automate repetitive business processes. My AI work includes agents, MCP servers, RAG and LLM integrations that connect models to application data and business tools, with appropriate permissions and approval for sensitive actions.

My approach is results-first: agree on the outcome, define the scope, and build toward it in small, reviewable steps. This is the idea behind Results as a Service: focusing on what the software needs to accomplish, not just the features delivered.

I also bring experience in e-commerce software, including contributions to published apps used by merchants, catalog and order integrations, and marketplace connections. Beyond e-commerce, I have built management MVPs for clients in real estate and furniture.

I am based in Lahore, Pakistan, and hold a BS-IT (Hons) degree, equivalent to 16 years of education.
